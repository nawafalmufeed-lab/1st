Welcome to Riyadh

Riyadh, the vibrant capital of Saudi Arabia, is a stunning metropolis where ancient heritage meets futuristic innovation. Home to over 7 million people, this desert oasis has transformed into one of the Middle East's most dynamic cities.

Modern Marvel

The city's skyline is dominated by iconic structures like the Kingdom Centre and Al Faisaliyah Tower. Riyadh showcases cutting-edge architecture, luxurious shopping districts, and world-class dining experiences that attract visitors globally.

Cultural Heritage

Beyond its modern facade, Riyadh preserves rich Saudi traditions. The historic Diriyah district, Masmak Fortress, and bustling traditional souks offer glimpses into the kingdom's fascinating past and Bedouin culture.

Vision 2030

As part of Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030, Riyadh is evolving into a global hub for business, tourism, and entertainment. Major projects like NEOM and the Riyadh Metro are reshaping the city's future, making it a beacon of progress in the Arabian Peninsula.
