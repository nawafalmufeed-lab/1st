## King Fahd's Fountain - Jeddah

![King Fahd's Fountain in Jeddah](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8e/King_Fahd%27s_Fountain.jpg/1200px-King_Fahd%27s_Fountain.jpg)

King Fahd's Fountain, also known as the Jeddah Fountain, is the tallest fountain in the world. Located on the coast of Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, it was donated to the city by King Fahd. The fountain jets water up to 312 meters (1,024 feet) above the Red Sea, making it a prominent landmark visible throughout the city.
